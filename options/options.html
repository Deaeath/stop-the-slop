Stop The Slop

Two signals: a comment section that's dead relative to the view count, and commenters saying out loud that it's machine-made. Tune how hard each one bites.

Thresholds
Comment ratio

Judged against what a real channel of the same size gets, not one flat number — big audiences comment proportionally less, so a flat threshold flags everyone. Measured defaults: ~1.5% at 3k views, ~0.15% at 5M.
